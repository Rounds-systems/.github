● Why the name

Medicine happens on the walk.

rounds /raʊndz/ noun

The daily walk of doctors from bed to bed through a ward, to review every patient, decide together, and teach.

Every morning, in every hospital in Pakistan, doctors walk the ward together. They stop at each bed. They argue over a chest film, catch what one pair of eyes missed, and teach the house officer standing at the back.

It is the oldest network in medicine. We named ourselves after it, and then took it off the ward. Same colleagues. Same standards. No visiting hours.
